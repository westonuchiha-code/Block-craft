BLOCKCRAFT - PLAYTEST LIST

== START HERE: A 15-MINUTE TOUR OF WHAT IS NEW (new world, seed 12345) ==
Nobody has played any of this; it was all built and checked by scripts and screenshots. The sections below go into detail.
 1. You wake in Ironhold, in the March of Lormark. Sound is on (MENU -> SETTINGS -> SOUND if it annoys you).
    A notice says there is a letter in your pocket: it is in your JOURNAL and under QUESTS, and sends you to the tavern keeper.
 2. Walk to the plaza. USE the wooden notice board at its edge: date, ruler, notices, and a posted bounty.
 3. Find the house with two hanging lamps and a sign: the tavern (The Laughing Boar). Talk to the innkeeper: room, food, rumours.
 4. Open the JOURNAL tab on your sheet: the date, the next holiday, what you have been told.
 5. Open the map: the province name at the top, its border as a dotted line, places you were told about. Press REALM for the whole country.
 6. Find a farmer and BUY A HORSE (300 gold, so this one may have to wait until you have earned it). USE it to mount, SNEAK to get down.
 7. Ride out along a road. By day a pedlar may come walking the other way. Cross a province border: a notice appears.
 8. Follow a rumour to a delve gate (seed 12345: the Black Hideout, gate at x 100, z -198). Inside, the map becomes an automap.
 9. Come back to the village in the late afternoon: villagers drift into the tavern, and go home after dark.
10. Hit a villager, then visit another village in the same province: the guards there want you too. Pay, serve, or run.
11. Day 4 is the Merchants' Fair: a notice at dawn, shops a quarter cheaper for the day.

== TO LOOK AT WHEN YOU ARE BACK (built 2026-11-14 onward, checked by scripts and screenshots only) ==
Start a NEW world for all of this (old worlds keep the old low land and have no delves).

SOUND (new, and NOBODY HAS HEARD IT: it was built and checked by measurement only. MENU -> SETTINGS -> SOUND sets the volume or turns it off)
[ ] First impression with sound on: better, or annoying? Say which sounds are wrong; each is one line to retune or remove.
[ ] Footsteps on grass, stone, wood, sand, snow: right pace, right loudness?
[ ] Digging and breaking, placing, sword swing and hits, being hurt.
[ ] Wind outdoors, rain, drips underground: too much, too little?
[ ] Creature voices when they notice you, the discovery chime, the quest fanfare.
[ ] Do you want music as well (Daggerfall had a lot of it)?

PEOPLE (you said they felt like they were floating)
[ ] Villagers, bandits, cats and beasts now have a dark patch under them. Do they feel planted on the ground now?
[ ] Watch someone walk: do the feet still slide? Is the slower stroll right? Do they turn naturally?
[ ] Walk into a villager: they should step aside, not pass through you.
[ ] If they still feel off, try to say when: standing, walking, turning, on stairs, at night?

HELD ITEMS
[ ] Doors, windows and iron bars are now held as their flat picture (they were edge-on strips). Better?
[ ] Blocks, stairs, doors, beds, lanterns, food, ingots in the hand: do they sit right? Chairs and fences are thin and low.
[ ] The 3D arm when the hand is empty, and its punch.
[ ] Fire and lightning on the fist and along a sword: too much? Does the fire hide the blade?

THE LIFTED WORLD
[ ] Does the land feel the same as before, just higher? Sea level is 56, mountains reach about 115 (build ceiling 120).
[ ] How long does it take to dig down to iron (below 48), mithril (below 20), adamantite (below 10)? Too far?
[ ] GPU speed on the S21 Ultra and a PC (never measured on real hardware).

TAVERNS (new worlds only; seed 12345: The Laughing Boar in the spawn village, the house with two lamps hanging by its door)
[ ] Find the tavern by its two lamps. Does it read as a tavern, or just a bigger house? There is a hanging sign beside the lamps: use it to read the name.
[ ] Talk to the innkeeper: RENT A ROOM (8g, the spare bed), TRADE (hot food), ASK FOR NEWS (always has another rumour).
[ ] Come by in the late afternoon: about two villagers in three walk over and stand about inside until well after dark, then go home.
    Does the village feel more alive for it? Do they block the doorway or crowd badly?

SMOOTHNESS WHEN TRAVELLING (worth a look on the phone especially)
[ ] Walking, and above all riding, into country you have never seen used to freeze the game for a fraction of a second each time a
    new region began. That planning is now done in the background ahead of you. Ride in a straight line for a few minutes: is it
    smooth? (If you go faster than the planning, by wagon or a very long gallop, the old short freeze can still happen.)

THE OPENING LETTER, AND BOUNTY HUNTERS
[ ] A new world starts with a letter (journal + quest list) sending you to the tavern keeper, who feeds you, hands over 25 gold and
    points you at the notice board and the news. Is that the right amount of hand-holding? Is the wording right for the game?
[ ] If a province wants you for 150 gold or more (two assaults, or a murder), a bounty hunter comes for you every few minutes
    whenever you are outside a village, until you pay or serve. Killing him settles nothing. Fair, or too much?

RULERS, THE NOTICE BOARD, BLESSINGS, PEDLARS
[ ] Each province has a ruler (the border notice, the map panel and ASK FOR NEWS name them) and a rival neighbour that people gossip
    about. Flavour only so far. Do the names and titles sit right (Margrave Ansel, Baroness Sibyl, Lord Sheriff Leofric)?
[ ] NEW WORLDS: a notice board stands at the edge of each village plaza. USE it: the place, province, ruler, date and next holiday;
    READ THE NOTICES marks a place on your map once a day; POSTED BOUNTY is the ruler's price on the nearest barrow or delve, which you
    report to the village guard. It has three sheets of paper pinned to it. Does it read as a notice board from across the plaza?
[ ] Carry out a ruler's bounty: you become a Friend of that province (three: a Knight, fines there halved; six: its Champion),
    and every village of the province you have visited thinks a little better of you. Is that reward enough to care about?
[ ] A cleric will bless you for 25 gold: until the sunrise after next, you heal half a heart every ten seconds. Worth it? Too strong?
[ ] By day, stand near a road outside a village for a minute or two: a pedlar should come walking along it. Stop him and trade.
    Does he keep to the road? Is one at a time right, or should the roads be busier?

THE HORSE (every world; a farmer sells one for 300 gold)
[ ] Buy one (BUY A HORSE when talking to a farmer). Look at it standing: does it read as a horse? Screenshot in proof/horse.png.
[ ] USE it to mount. You sit 0.85 higher, and its head and ears show at the bottom of the view. Is the head in the way, or right?
[ ] Ride: 1.85x your speed (walking or running). Too fast, too slow? You cannot ride through a normal doorway.
[ ] SNEAK (Z or R3) gets you down; the horse waits there and is marked on the map. Is sneak the right button for it?
[ ] Third person in the saddle: do rider and horse look right together, turning, moving?
[ ] It cannot be hurt. Wrong? (It also cannot be stolen, fed, or named.)

CALENDAR AND HOLIDAYS (every world)
[ ] The journal now shows dates (a new world wakes on the 1st of Greening, year 1) and tells you the next holiday.
[ ] The 4th day of a new world is the Merchants' Fair: a notice at dawn, and everything in the shops a quarter cheaper for the day.
    Others through the year: free temple healing, half-price lessons, a free bed and better crop prices, half-cost spells,
    halved fines, half-fare wagons. Villagers mention a holiday when it is within four days.
[ ] Are eight holidays in a 96-day year too many, too few? Do the month names sit right (Frostwane, Seedfall, Greening, Highsun,
    Goldleaf, Harvestmoon, Emberfall, Deepwinter)?

WHAT PEOPLE SAY
[ ] Villagers now often open with something about the moment (night, rain, a holiday, your horse, your wounds, your honours, how the
    village feels about you), and ASK FOR NEWS sometimes brings gossip about a named neighbour. Read a few: does the voice fit the
    game? Any line that grates, tell me which and it goes.

THE REALM MAP (every world)

    LOCAL goes back to the ordinary map. Screenshot: proof/realm_map.png.
[ ] Does it give the sense of a big country? Should unvisited provinces be hidden until you hear of them, as a discovery?

PROVINCES (every world, old or new)
[ ] The land is now divided into named baronies, counties and marches about 1,400 blocks across. Open the map: the name is at the
    top and the border is a faint dotted line. Walk (or take a wagon) across one: a notice, and a line in the journal.
[ ] ASK FOR NEWS: people now say which province their village stands in.
[ ] Hit someone in one village, then visit another village in the same province: its guards should want you too (for half as much).
    Theft stays local. Too harsh, or right?

DELVES (seed 12345: the Black Hideout, a bandit den, gate at x 100, z -198, about 250 blocks north of spawn)
[ ] USE a post of a delve's gate with a tool or empty hand: an inscription tells you what the place was, how many chambers lie
    below and what holds it now. At the very bottom, USE the altar stone: its story, and the whole plan appears on your map.
    Do the inscriptions add to the place, or get in the way of building against the gate?
[ ] Finding the gate; the walk down the stair.
[ ] Size and mood of the caverns; is the glowing moss too much or too little light?
[ ] Can you find your way to the shrine? Is it too easy, too confusing, too long?

[ ] Digging shortcuts: fun or does it spoil it?
[ ] Open the MAP underground: it becomes an automap of the delve. Does it help without giving too much away?

JOURNAL AND QUESTS
[ ] Character sheet -> JOURNAL: is the record useful? Anything missing?
[ ] The three delve jobs from villagers: kill the master, bring back the heirloom, rescue someone lost. Pay and time limit fair?
[ ] Rescue: does the follower keep up through tunnels and up the stair, or get stuck? (Never watched by a person.)

THE HERMIT (in the Black Hideout: chamber 6, a camp with a bedroll and a lamp on a barrel)

ARTIFACTS (most delve shrines hold one in the first chest: Emberbrand, Stormcaller, Lifedrinker, Deepdelver)

GUILDS (ask a guard, a scholar or a cleric to join; see STANDING on the sheet)
