BLOCKCRAFT 1985 - FEATURE NOTES
Prototype as of 2026-11-08
One biome only. Terrain varies in height: grass, sand near low ground, and trees.

For each feature, write what you'd change under "What I'd change".


1. WORLD AND TERRAIN

Endless seeded world
  Now: Generated from a seed in 16x16 chunks, radius 4 around the player. Distant chunks are unloaded.
  What I'd change:

World height
  Now: 32 blocks. Surface is about 10 to 22 blocks up.
  What I'd change:

Terrain layers
  Now: Grass on top, 3 layers of dirt, then stone. Sand where ground is low.
  What I'd change:

Gravel patches
  Now: Scattered in the dirt layer.
  What I'd change:

Coal ore
  Now: About 4% of stone.
  What I'd change:

Trees
  Now: About 3% of grass tiles. 4-block trunk with a leafy top.
  What I'd change:

Dungeon halls
  Now: About half of each 32x32 region has one. 3x3 inside, cobblestone walls, glowstone lamps every 6 blocks, 12 to 23 blocks long, underground at y 3 to 5.
  What I'd change:

Edit memory
  Now: Digging and building are kept while the page is open, even after chunks unload.
  What I'd change:

Saving
  Now: None. Refreshing the page wipes all changes.
  What I'd change:


2. BLOCKS AND TEXTURES

Block types
  Now: Grass, dirt, stone, cobblestone, sand, gravel, log, planks, leaves, glass, glowstone, coal ore. Coal is also an item.
  What I'd change:

Textures
  Now: Procedural 16x16 patterns: grass lip, stone cracks, cobblestone mortar, plank seams, log rings and bark, leaf holes, glass frame, glowstone and coal spots.
  What I'd change:

Held-block textures
  Now: The held block uses the same textures as world blocks.
  What I'd change:


3. PLAYER AND INTERACTION

Movement
  Now: First-person. Walk or run (toggle), jump, gravity, collision with blocks.
  What I'd change:

Walk and run
  Now: RUN button or Q toggles run speed. Stick is full speed past a small dead zone.
  What I'd change:

Break
  Now: 6-block reach. No tools or break time. Bottom layer can't be broken.
  What I'd change:

Break drops
  Now: Grass gives dirt. Stone gives cobblestone. Coal ore gives coal. Leaves give nothing. Everything else gives itself.
  What I'd change:

Place
  Now: From the hotbar. Can't place inside the player.
  What I'd change:

Hotbar
  Now: 9 slots, keys 1 to 9 or tap. Counts only, no stack limit.
  What I'd change:

Starting inventory
  Now: 20 dirt, 8 logs, 10 stone, 4 glowstone.
  What I'd change:

Crafting
  Now: One recipe (C key or CRAFT button): 1 log gives 4 planks. No crafting table or other recipes.
  What I'd change:

Survival
  Now: No health, hunger, fall damage, or death.
  What I'd change:


4. LIGHTING AND SKY

Day and night
  Now: Day lasts 30 minutes. Night lasts 10 minutes.
  What I'd change:

Sun
  Now: Moves across the sky and casts hard shadows. Trees and hills block it.
  What I'd change:

Moon
  Now: Dimmer light at night.
  What I'd change:

Face shading
  Now: Faces are shaded by their angle to the light.
  What I'd change:

Caves
  Now: Dark unless glowstone is nearby.
  What I'd change:

Glowstone light
  Now: Fades over about 15 blocks, only within its own chunk.
  What I'd change:

Sky color
  Now: Shifts from day to night. Distant blocks fade into the horizon color beyond about 40 blocks.
  What I'd change:

Not drawn
  Now: Clouds, a visible sun or moon, stars.
  What I'd change:


5. HELD ITEM AND ANIMATION

Empty hand
  Now: Short arm in the bottom-right corner.
  What I'd change:

Holding a block
  Now: Textured block in the bottom-right corner. Hand is hidden.
  What I'd change:

Punch
  Now: Hand or block drives toward the center of the screen when you break or place.
  What I'd change:

Walking bob
  Now: Arm bobs slightly up and down while walking.
  What I'd change:


6. HUD AND CONTROLS

HUD
  Now: Position, block you're looking at, chunk count, day or night, coal, planks, and log counts.
  What I'd change:

Desktop controls
  Now: WASD move. Space jump. Arrow keys look. Mouse look after clicking to lock the pointer. Left click break. Right click place. C craft. Q run. 1 to 9 hotbar.
  What I'd change:

Touch controls
  Now: Left stick to move. Drag to look. Buttons for JUMP, BREAK, PLACE, CRAFT, RUN. Tap the hotbar. Hidden on desktop.
  What I'd change:

Layout
  Now: Game view on the top half, controls on the bottom half on phones.
  What I'd change:

Render resolution
  Now: Fixed at 224x140, scaled up.
  What I'd change:
